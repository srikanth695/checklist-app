🛡️ Privacy Policy & GDPR Compliance

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Checklist App ("we", "us", "our") operates in compliance with the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data.

2. Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Username (optional)
  • First and last name (optional)
  • Password hash (for email/password authentication only)
  • Profile picture URL (for OAuth users)

User-Generated Content

  • Habits and habit completion history
  • Goals and progress tracking
  • Journal entries and reflections
  • Schedule events and tasks
  • Daily metrics and mood data

Technical Information

  • IP address (for security and audit logging)
  • Login timestamps
  • User agent information
  • Account activity logs

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Processing Activity Legal Basis Description
Account Creation & Management Contractual Necessity Processing is necessary to provide the service you've requested
Authentication & Security Legitimate Interest Protecting user accounts and preventing unauthorized access
Audit & Compliance Logging Legal Obligation & Legitimate Interest Maintaining records for legal compliance and security
Marketing Communications Consent Only sent with explicit user opt-in
Platform Improvements Legitimate Interest Analyzing usage patterns to improve our service

4. Your GDPR Rights

🔍 Right to Access (Article 15)

You have the right to request a copy of all personal data we hold about you in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. Use the "Export My Data" feature in your account settings.

✏️ Right to Rectification (Article 16)

You can request correction of inaccurate personal data. Update your profile information in account settings at any time.

🗑️ Right to Erasure (Article 17)

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data. We will permanently delete your data within 30 days of receiving a verified deletion request. Use "Delete Account" in settings.

⏸️ Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18)

You can restrict how your data is processed. Contact us with specific processing restrictions.

📤 Right to Data Portability (Article 20)

You can receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format and transfer it to another service. Use the "Export My Data" feature.

🚫 Right to Object (Article 21)

You can object to processing for legitimate interests. Manage your preferences in account settings or contact us.

🤖 Rights Related to Automated Decisions (Article 22)

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

🔐 Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7)

For any processing based on consent (e.g., marketing emails), you can withdraw consent anytime. Manage in account settings.

5. Data Retention

  • Active Account Data: Retained for the duration of your account
  • Deleted Accounts: Permanently deleted within 30 days of deletion request
  • Audit Logs: Retained for 90 days for security purposes
  • Backup Data: May be retained for up to 90 days for disaster recovery

6. Data Security

  • ✅ Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (PBKDF2)
  • ✅ All data transmitted over HTTPS encrypted connections
  • ✅ Database-level access controls and encryption at rest
  • ✅ Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • ✅ Audit logging of all data access and modifications
  • ✅ No data shared with third parties except OAuth providers

7. Third-Party Services

🔷 Google OAuth

When you sign in with Google, we collect your Google profile information. Google processes this data according to their privacy policy. Data shared: email, name, profile picture.

8. Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

By creating an account, you agree to our Data Processing Agreement which outlines:

  • Explicit consent to process personal data for service provision
  • Purpose limitation (data used only for providing the service)
  • Data minimization (we collect only necessary data)
  • Storage limitation (data deleted as specified in retention policy)
  • Integrity and confidentiality (data protected and secured)

9. Contact & Complaints

Data Protection Inquiries

For any GDPR-related questions, data access requests, or to exercise your rights, contact us at: privacy@checklistapp.com

Supervisory Authority

If you believe we have violated your GDPR rights, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (DPA).

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and requesting your renewed consent if necessary.
